# DraftCraft trademark assignment

This page is the repository's record of the transfer of the DraftCraft trademark. The brand terms that
follow from it are in [`LICENSE-brand.txt`](LICENSE-brand.txt).

- **Date:** 19 November 2026.
- **From:** WithShalom Studios ([withshalom](https://github.com/withshalom)). It created the DraftCraft
  name, the DraftCraft logo and the DraftCraft app icon, and first used them and claimed DraftCraft as
  its trademark (2026).
- **To:** DraftCraft Engineering Ventures ([DraftCraft-DEV](https://github.com/DraftCraft-DEV)), the
  organization that makes DraftCraft.
- **What is transferred:** WithShalom Studios' claimed trademark rights in the DraftCraft name
  (DraftCraft™, also written DRAFTCRAFT or Draftcraft), the DraftCraft logo and the DraftCraft app icon,
  as `LICENSE-brand.txt` lists them, together with the goodwill that goes with them.

From this date the marks belong to DraftCraft Engineering Ventures, whose brand terms
(`LICENSE-brand.txt`) replace the terms WithShalom Studios gave. The permission WithShalom Studios had
given this repository and SkryxDev to use the marks ends; the organization's own use takes its place.

What does not change:

- The code's licence (MIT OR Apache-2.0) and its copyright notices (`LICENSE-MIT`, `NOTICE`).
- The credits: SkryxDev for RustDraft and his work in DraftCraft, WithShalom Studios for creating the
  name, the logo and the app icon (`README.md`, `ATTRIBUTION.md`).

The ™ sign marks a claimed trademark. Nothing here is a registration.
